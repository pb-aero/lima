RV-8 cockpit interface board: test points

What each port of the GMA 245R ↔ ADAU1860 interface board should read, and what fails it. Print it and fill in the blank column. Run the build sheet's power-off resistance and isolation checks first. This sheet starts once those pass.

Every expected value is worked from the build sheet's own figures (−8.3 dBFS at 3.12 V rms; 0.22 V rms at 0 dBFS; codec full scale 0.98 V rms; P30 legs at 0.85 V DC). The rms → peak-to-peak conversions assume a sine. Nothing on this page has been measured yet. The blank column is where that starts.

Before probing
  • Scope ground is a wire across the isolation line. Never clip one probe's ground on the aircraft side (TB1, J1, J2) and another on our side (P11, P30) at the same time. Use a differential probe, or measure one side per session with a battery-powered meter.
  • Never ground a P30 leg, and that includes a scope ground clip. P30 is a bridge output. Measure tip and ring against DEV-B ground with the probe tips only, or differentially.
  • In the aircraft: DEV-B and any laptop on battery, no PTT, and talk only after Duran's yes.

What shouldn't be on either path

  • Pitch that tracks engine RPM: alternator whine coupling in. Clip the ferrites on the leads at the box first.
  • Steady buzz or a fixed tone: DEV-B's own digital noise. Check that P11's shield reaches the sleeve, and that DEV-B is on battery.
  • A DC step on P11 when audio starts or stops: a leak across the isolation line. Stop and recheck the power-off isolation.
  • Flat-topped peaks: clipping. At the panel's 3.12 V rms maximum, listen still has about 8 dB of headroom, so clipping on P11 points to DEV-B's input gain, not the panel. Talk is capped by R14.
  • Steady test tone fading on talk: not a fault. The panel's voice gate learns steady tones as noise. Use speech or broken tones.

Notes and what isn't known

  • The TY-145P's rated frequency range was not checked. It's a 600 Ω telephone-grade part, which is why the listen sweep row records the −3 dB points rather than passing or failing them. That matters if prop tones are wanted in the recording: a 2-blade prop at 2,400 rpm is about 80 Hz.
  • The talk path's low-frequency corner (C1 with R4 and the panel's 150 Ω) is estimated at around 60 Hz, which is fine for voice. That's an estimate, not a figure from the build sheet.
  • The ±3 dB windows are the build sheet's gate, converted to volts here. The P30 DC tolerance (about 0.1 V) is this sheet's suggestion. The build sheet gives only 0.85 V.
