Pin positions are the real pads from the KiCad layouts. Wire colours are for telling conductors apart here; the harness colour code isn't chosen yet. The AeroNode is the Rev 0.0.2 board model (updated GPS), with the flat cable landing on its 24-way Cvilux J9; which end of J9 is contact 1 is assumed. The AeroIMU Rev 0 and AeroVault Rev 0.0.2 are their board models; the battery pack is still a stand-in. J11/J12/J2 signals come from the AeroNode schematic. Cable routes and lengths are schematic.
