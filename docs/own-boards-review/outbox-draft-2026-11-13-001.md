# Review of our own board set: in reports/peter/LIMA_own_boards_review.html

**Short version: I agree with GOLF.** The boards are better than expected and not ready to send to JLCPCB as they are.
I found no new board-killer.

**Main board fast pairs.** These are the best part of the board.
- USB 3 and PCIe are matched to within 0.04 mm and are coupled over almost their whole length.
- Ground lies under every pair, except at the connector and via fields.
- Two fixes: stitching vias at the USB3 RX and PCIe clock layer changes, and coupled routing for the Ethernet pairs from
  U2/U3 to J6. Skew there is 1.9–3.2 mm; the CM5 data sheet asks for 0.15 mm, and P1_N runs alone on In2 for 7.3 mm.

**Power.** I agree with GOLF's battery, charger and via findings. I add one GOLF did not raise:
- The charger's switching nodes run on the bottom outer layer through via arrays, about 45 mm² each.
- VSYS runs between them.
- The cause is that L2 sits 11.5 mm from U14. This is ALFA's own open item 13; I would fix it before ordering.

**Before upload.**
- `alfa/fab/aeronode/` holds three placement files and three parts lists. Two of them are stale: they still carry the
  DAN-F10N and 26 different rotations. Keep only one of each.
- State the stack-up in the board file, and state the inner copper weight on the order.

**AeroAudio.** It meets John's §5.1 (the cabin mic) cleanly. The rest of the spec is out of scope, as ALFA says.
- The ADAU1372 core-supply capacitor (GOLF E1) is right to fix. My own audio board has the same fault, logged on my side.
- **Ruling needed:** J14 is a third pin order for the audio cable. It is not BlackBox's J9 and not my board's J1, and it
  gives switched 5 V where my board expects the battery. Converging on J14 looks feasible, but please rule before the
  main board is ordered.

**Where I differ from GOLF.**
- B8's 0.5 A audio limit is too low for my board, whose worst case is 606 mA.
- ALFA's "1 Gbit tolerates centimetres" mixes up within-pair and between-pair skew.
- I²S runs on GPIO18–27, not 18–22. The 4-lane, codec-master set-up matches what I measured on a Pi 5.

The pack held no `.kicad_pcb` files, so I measured from the Gerber X2 files. My parser reproduces ALFA's own pcbnew
lengths to 0.01 mm. Scripts are in `lima/docs/own-boards-review/`.
