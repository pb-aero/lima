SHEET 1December demonstrator — front end and interconnect

DVNC on EVB 1 into the left cup, ANC on EVB 2 into the right cup. Each technology is independently audible, independently measurable, and independently debuggable — and a listener A/Bs either one by covering an ear.

Sheet 1 — the two numbers that decide whether the demo reads as a success

  1. The feed-forward PGA stays at 0 dB. The codec clips before the microphone does: 0.98 V rms differential full scale ÷ 12.6 mV/Pa = 131.8 dB SPL, against the IM73A135's own 135 dB SPL acoustic overload point. A 100 dB cockpit lands at −31.8 dBFS, which is ample — there is no reason to reach for PGA gain and good reason not to.
  2. The microphone's phase response must be calibrated out. The IM73A135 is +12° at 75 Hz. Uncorrected, that alone caps cancellation at 2·sin(6°) = 0.209, i.e. −13.6 dB — and it lands exactly on prop blade-pass. It is specified, repeatable, and the sensitivity tolerance is ±1 dB, so it is correctable in the DSP. If it is not corrected, the demo will look like the technology does not work.
Correction to what I said earlier: I claimed both boards default to I²C 0x64 so one must be re-strapped. That is only true if they share a bus. Each EVAL-ADAU1860EBZ carries its own FTDI and its own USB connection, so on the bench they never meet and 0x64 on both is fine. The S14 strap (0x64/0x65/0x66/0x67) matters later, when something like a CM5 drives both over one bus. [gap] whether Lark Studio will talk to two boards at once, or wants two instances.
[gap] Check before adding C3–C11: the EVB already fits an input network on P9/P10/P11. If coupling capacitors are already populated there, the ones drawn here put two in series and shift the corner. Read the board before soldering.
[gap] Pin numbers are not drawn for U1, MK1–MK3 and U2. The specifications above were read from the datasheets this session; the pinouts were not. Confirm them before any breakout is fabricated — the ADXL354 breakout is the one board on this sheet that has to be made.
[gap] The earcup is the biggest unknown on this sheet. If the target headset already has its own ANR, the demonstrator is two cancellers fighting in one cup, which performs worse than either alone. The demo needs a passive cup, or one with its ANR defeated. Decide this before ordering anything.

SHEET 2Aircraft-audio summing amplifier — product side

Not needed for December. This is the circuit that puts AeroNode's mono DAC onto the intercom's earphone lines. It is a series insertion: the panel becomes a 10 kΩ input, the summing node is a virtual ground, and the earphone sees only the amplifier's output.

Sheet 2 — what the topology buys, and what it costs

  • ANC authority stops depending on the panel. The panel drives a 10 kΩ input instead of a 160 Ω earphone, so anti-noise at the ear goes from ~40.5 mV worst case to the DAC's full 1.0 V rms — about +28 dB, and the panel's output impedance drops out of the answer entirely.
  • The summing resistors are the codec protection. With U1 unpowered the panel reaches HPOUT through the R20/R21 divider: 2.83 V pk × ½ = 1.41 V pk, inside the ADAU1860's 2.1 V pin limit. That is derived, not measured — it needs a bench check with the rail pulled.
  • The cost is headroom, and it is the open question. The op-amp must now produce the panel's full level, coherently summed with the anti-noise — and DVNC's sustained tones have no crest factor to hide behind. A 2 V rms panel needs 4.24 V peak; ±5 V gives 4.4 V at the OPA1664's 2 kΩ swing spec, which is not margin at 320 Ω.
[gap] The measurement that gates this whole sheet: the panel's open-circuit output voltage and its output impedance. Open-circuit, not loaded — this circuit presents 10 kΩ where the earphones present 160 Ω, so the panel will read higher into it. The voltage decides the rail (±5 V versus a 12 V boost); the impedance decided the authority argument above. Ten minutes on a real aircraft with a scope and a resistor.
[gap] Ruling needed: a summer mixes AeroNode's voice with the radio rather than replacing it, so K1 stops being the changeover it was ruled to be and becomes a fail-passive bypass only. That is a behaviour change, not a component change.
